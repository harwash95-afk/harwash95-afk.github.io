Turning Plans Into Delivered Projects

Houssam Harwash is a project management and marketing professional based in Toronto, Canada. His career spans three countries and two industries, and one rule runs through all of it: plan it properly, then execute it precisely.

Today his focus is project management, operations, and marketing, with a standing commitment to humanitarian work.

The Journey

Three Cities, One Direction

It Started With Helping People

He studied economics at Damascus University. Then the Syrian revolution began, and families around him lost everything overnight.

He did not wait for an organization or a plan. He started himself. He found houses for families who had nowhere to sleep, and he put food and medicine in their hands, funded by private people who trusted him to get it there.

Helping people is not something he did once. It is who he is, then and now, and it still drives his humanitarian work wherever in the world it is needed.

Saudi Arabia / Aramco

For five years he lived and worked in the Eastern Region of Saudi Arabia, based in Khobar, as Projects and Planning Division Manager at Abrican for Oil and Gas Services.

His work ran through Saudi Aramco: projects, tenders, and purchase orders for Aramco contracts around Dhahran, handled in Aramco's own systems, to Aramco's standard.

Hard work gets noticed. Aramco's South Ghawar Maintenance Services Division awarded him its Letter of Appreciation.

Canada, Built on Credentials

Advanced Diploma in Business Administration from Sheridan College, High Honour Graduate with a 3.93 GPA, and a first place finish in a strategic business simulation ranked in the Global Top 50.

PMP®. Chartered Marketer. Licensed Ontario realtor. And in 2019, co-founder of Harwash Inc in Toronto, where he leads operations, marketing, and business development.
